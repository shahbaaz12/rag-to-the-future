Nexa SecureCam Door
Product ID: NEX-1004
Category: Battery video doorbell

DESCRIPTION
A weather-resistant video doorbell with motion alerts, two-way audio, local event history, and optional cloud backup. It is intended for a front door or protected outdoor entrance with a reliable Wi-Fi signal.

KEY FEATURES
- 1080p live video, night vision, and adjustable motion zones.
- Rechargeable battery with low-battery notifications.
- Two-way talk from the Nexa Home mobile app.

SETUP
- Charge the unit fully before mounting it at chest height beside the door.
- Add SecureCam in the app while standing near the installation location.
- Set motion zones to exclude public roads or constantly moving trees.

HOW TO USE
- Tap a notification to open live view or speak with a visitor.
- Review Events to see clips sorted by date and motion type.
- Charge when the app reports 20 percent battery or less.

ERROR CODES AND RESOLUTIONS
E501: Camera offline. Resolution: Check Wi-Fi strength at the door, then press and hold the side button for 10 seconds.
E514: Storage unavailable. Resolution: Confirm the local storage card is inserted correctly or free space in cloud storage.
E527: Motion zone invalid. Resolution: Redraw the zone so it remains inside the visible camera frame.
