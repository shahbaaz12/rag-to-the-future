Nexa Glow Bulb A19
Product ID: NEX-1003
Category: Tunable smart LED bulb

DESCRIPTION
An energy-efficient A19 LED bulb with adjustable brightness and white color temperature. It is designed for lamps and standard indoor fixtures, with controls from the Nexa Home app, schedules, or a paired HomeHub.

KEY FEATURES
- 800-lumen output with warm-to-cool white adjustment.
- Dimmable in the app without a separate dimmer switch.
- Schedules, room groups, and energy-use estimates.

SETUP
- Turn off fixture power, install the bulb, then restore power.
- Wait for the bulb to blink twice, then select Add Device in the app.
- Name the bulb and assign it to a room before creating schedules.

HOW TO USE
- Use the Brightness slider for light level and the Temperature slider for tone.
- Group bulbs by room to control several fixtures together.
- Use a wake-up routine to increase brightness gradually in the morning.

ERROR CODES AND RESOLUTIONS
E401: Bulb not discovered. Resolution: Cycle fixture power three times, wait for a blink, and begin pairing again.
E407: Unsupported wall dimmer detected. Resolution: Use a standard on/off switch; do not place the bulb on a legacy dimmer.
E415: Firmware update interrupted. Resolution: Keep power on, remain on Wi-Fi, and retry the update from Device Settings.
