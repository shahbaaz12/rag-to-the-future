Nexa LeakGuard
Product ID: NEX-1010
Category: Water-leak sensor

DESCRIPTION
A small battery-powered sensor that detects water beneath sinks, washing machines, water heaters, and similar risk areas. It sends an immediate alert to the Nexa Home app and can trigger a compatible shutoff valve.

KEY FEATURES
- Water-contact detection and loud local alarm.
- Temperature warning for potential pipe-freeze conditions.
- Optional automation with a compatible smart shutoff valve.

SETUP
- Place the metal contact points flat on a dry surface near, not inside, the likely leak path.
- Add LeakGuard in the app and label it with its exact location.
- Test once by touching a few drops of clean water to the contacts, then dry them.

HOW TO USE
- Treat every water alert as urgent and inspect the named location first.
- Use event history to confirm when the sensor became wet or dry.
- Replace the battery annually or whenever the low-battery alert appears.

ERROR CODES AND RESOLUTIONS
E1101: Sensor wet. Resolution: Inspect for a leak immediately, dry the contacts, and resolve the source before resetting.
E1110: Battery low. Resolution: Replace the coin-cell battery with the specified type and retest the sensor.
E1124: Temperature reading unavailable. Resolution: Remove the battery for 30 seconds, reinstall it, and check the device connection.
